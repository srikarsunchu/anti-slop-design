# Rage feed — the category's cleanest quote wall, and what Spite took from it

Source: App Store listing id6771587954 (Rage - Motivational Quotes, CutCoach
Inc.), six listing screenshots read 2026-09-05. No live DOM; values are
read from screenshots and marked as such. DAWG (id6742336314) read the same
day for contrast.

## The one idea
"Motivation without the BS." The thing that embodies it is the feed: a black
screen, one line of text, three glyphs at the bottom. No card, no photo, no
chrome. The notification screenshot leads the listing, so the second idea
is "it comes to you".

## Color
- ground: #000 (pure black; the listing frames and the feed agree)
- surfaces: topic rows are #1A1A1A pills with a 1px lighter border; tiles for Favourites / Collections / My own quotes / History
- ink / secondary / tertiary: white / ~60% white / ~35% white
- accent: none in the product. The only colour on any screen is the App
  Store's blue and the five gold stars in the listing badge.
- count of distinct colors: greys only.

## Ground
Pure black in the feed. The wallpapers and the listing use monochrome
photographs of bodybuilders and classical statues, desaturated, with the
subject dark and centred and the line over the chest or feet.

## Icons
Outline set, ~24, stroke ~1.75: share (arrow out of tray), bookmark,
heart, and a flame on each topic row. Looks like Feather / Lucide.

## Layout
- feed: line centred both ways; three glyphs in a row at the bottom, evenly spaced, no labels
- topics: full page, "Explore topics" title, a 2×2 tile grid (Favourites,
  Collections, My own quotes, History), "Most Popular" section, rows with a
  flame icon and chevron, search at the bottom
- wallpapers: 3-column grid of photos, an "Aa" badge on each meaning the
  line can be set over it
- edges ~20; radii 12 on rows and tiles

## Hierarchy read
The line is the subject by isolation, not by size; it is small type on a
big void. That is the opening Spite takes: same isolation, but the line at
84pt in Anton so the type becomes the wall.

## The lesson, as a rule
"A quote app is one screen, so give it no tab bar; the line is the product,
so make the line the biggest thing on the screen and let everything else be a
sheet."

Second lesson: "The category's photos are its tell and its rights problem;
grain over near-black is a ground that costs nothing and reads as the same
genre."

Third, from DAWG: "Gamification is a different product. A streak number is
all a quote app should borrow from it."

## What Spite copied, value for value
- structure: feed, topics with padlocks, saved, wallpapers, reminder, widget
- prices: $3.99 a week, $29.99 a year
- the three glyphs at the bottom (share, heart; Spite drops bookmark
  because heart and bookmark were the same action)
- the listing order: notification first

## What Spite changed
- type: Anton at 46 to 84pt, left-set, uppercase; Rage centres 17pt
- ground: #0A0A0A with a radial lift and a 5% grain tile; Rage is #000
- one accent, red, for the streak number and the padlock; Rage has none
- content: 200 original second-person lines with no attribution; Rage
  attributes to Goggins, Tyson, Jocko
