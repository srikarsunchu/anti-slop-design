# Clipping agencies — the category's landing page, measured

Source: eight live sites captured 2026-09-08 at 1280 wide (Lumina Clippers, ClipUp, Vision Clipping, clippingservices.net, clippingagency.co, Clipmasters, ShortVids, FORKOFF) plus the Whop store pages of Propaganda (274k members) and Cliptic (79k). Computed styles read from the DOM on Lumina and ClipUp.

## The one idea
"Proof of distribution." Every page is a number and a face: how many views this operator has moved, and who moved them. The thing that embodies it is the founder video with the views total burned into its thumbnail (Lumina: "18B+ VIEWS VIA CLIPS" over the founder's face).

CTA: pill (radius 46px Lumina, 999px ClipUp), 18px on Lumina, 13.6px on ClipUp. Lumina's accent is a green sampled from its logo, used on the CTA fill and one glow; ClipUp uses a periwinkle blue on numbers only.

## Ground
Lumina: near-black with a soft green glow behind the hero and a full-bleed video thumbnail. clippingagency/clippingservices: a collage of real clip frames (faces, captions, platform icons) behind the headline. ClipUp: warm off-white with a video card. Nobody uses abstract tiles.

## Whop side
The network's Whop page is a feed of recruiting posts ("LOOKING FOR X/TWITTER CLIPPERS", polls, "applications close in 12 hours") and a sidebar of apps (Discord, Deal Tracker, Content Rewards, Clipping Course, Livestreams, "Launch your clipping campaign"). Identity is the founder: "Created by Eddie Cumberbatch."

## The lesson, as a rule
A clipping agency page is a person plus a counter plus named clients with views. Build the slots for those three, seed them with specifics, and let the deployer fill them; without them any layout reads as a template.

## Confidence
Frequencies and type values are measured. Conversion effects are not; see the CRO notes in the sports-picks research for those.
