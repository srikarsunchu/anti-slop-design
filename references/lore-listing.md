## The lesson as a rule

A screenshot row is one composition seen as thumbnails, so give each slide
one flat colour from a small family, one serif claim, and one stylised phone,
and stop at four or five. The phone's bezel, not a shadow or a wave, is what
separates it from the ground.

Applied to Swaddle's store shots 2026-09-05: night-time pastels, Fraunces
headline, white-bezel phone, the dark player inside.
