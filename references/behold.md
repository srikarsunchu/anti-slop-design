# Behold: Experience Islam (iOS, App Store listing and in-app screens)

Digested 2026-09-04 from the US App Store screenshots (id 6741750934). Sri
pointed at it as the answer to "nothing about this dark UI says who it is for."

## What it is

A daily-practice app. Every screen is an encounter with one piece of content:
a verse, a dhikr, an episode, the prayer times. Dark, editorial, warm.

## Ground truth (measured from the screenshots)

- Ground: pure black (#000), 38 to 42 percent of every frame. Not #111.
- Colour: one photographic or gradient field per item, fading into the black
  by mid-screen. Fields measured: amber sunset (#B06008, #A05808), sienna
  (#482818, #381810), dusk blue (#182838, #304058), slate (#606068). Never a
  flat tint; always a field with depth, and it belongs to the content, never
  to the chrome.
- Type: white sans for UI, large light sans for the item title ("Anxiety" at
  roughly 64px), mono caps for machinery (DHIKR · 1 OF 3, 57 AYAHS · 35M).
- Photographs of real objects: a vase, a staircase, a bookshelf. Dim, warm,
  editorial. The objects carry the mood; the UI stays quiet.
- Listing: black frames, headline in sentence case at the top, one straight-on
  device, no angled phones, no wave shapes.

## The lesson as a rule

A dark UI says who it is for through its content field, not its greys. Give
every item one photographed or painted colour field that fades into the
ground, keep the chrome black and quiet, and let the object in the photograph
name the audience. Recolouring the chrome, light or dark, does not do it.

Applied in Swaddle: black ground, one dim photograph per sound family (fan,
hearth, drum, window, rain, muslin, bedroom), lamp-glow over it, Fraunces for
the sound's name, "Ada · 6 weeks" as the line that makes it hers.

## Confidence

Colours measured from downscaled screenshots (8-bit buckets). Type sizes
estimated from the 1290-wide frames. Not verified in the app itself.
