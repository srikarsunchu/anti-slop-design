# Landing pages and marketing sites

## Anatomy of a full site (the depth bar)

1. Nav: logo, three to five links, one CTA. Sticky, blurred ground.
2. Hero: eyebrow, headline under 12 words, two-sentence subhead, two CTAs,
   and the anchor: the product doing its job on real data, over a rich ground.
3. Stats band: three big numbers with one line of context each.
4. How it works: three cards, each with a mini demo that moves.
5. Full-width product demo: a table, a timeline, a schedule, with real rows.
6. Setup steps: three numbered, each with a command or a mini visual.
7. Integrations: names or real logos. Never hand-drawn logos.
8. Social proof if it is real. Skip if it is not.
9. FAQ: accordion with grid-rows animation.
10. Closing CTA over the ground again.
11. Footer: quiet.

Budget 8 to 12. At least three sections move. The product is shown working
in most.

## The hero decides everything

- The anchor is the product, not an illustration. A real input with a real
  placeholder query. A real file with a real diff. A real table mid-task.
- The ground is a photo, a dither, a mesh, or a texture. Clean UI floats over
  it. Sample the accent from the ground if there is one.
- Text over the ground gets a contrast check.
- Entrance: the anchor rises 24px over 800ms strong ease-out. Nothing else
  animates on load.

## Type on landing pages

Display at 45 to 56px, weight 500 or 600, tracking −0.025em. A serif display
reads editorial and human; a heavy sans reads punchy. Pick from the idea.
Body 14 to 16. Eyebrow in mono caps.
