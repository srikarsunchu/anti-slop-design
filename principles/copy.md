# Copy

Generated pages are given away by their words as often as their pixels.

## Voice

- Short sentences. One idea each.
- Specific nouns. "Scene 14 ran long" beats "stay on schedule".
- Name the alternative. "Not a spreadsheet you stop updating at lunch."
  "Not a stale database." The "X. Not Y." snap.
- Name competitors when it is true and useful.
- Numbers with context, not adjectives. "112 of 118 shots covered" beats
  "comprehensive coverage".

## Words that mark a page as generated

Seamless, effortless, powerful, robust, unlock, supercharge, elevate,
streamline, leverage, empower, cutting-edge, next-generation, all-in-one,
revolutionize, "in today's fast-paced world". Delete on sight.

## Structure

- Eyebrow: mono caps, three to five words, tells the reader where they are.
- Headline: one idea, under 12 words, no widow on the last line.
- Subhead: two sentences, the second one concrete.
- CTA: a verb and an object. "Start a project", "See a shoot day". Not
  "Get started" and "Learn more".

## Demo content

Every mock needs a scenario a person could have lived. Names that sound like
people, times that add up, file names with real extensions, prices that are
not $99. Write the scenario first, then fill the table from it.

## Rule

Read the page aloud. If any sentence could appear on any other product's
site, rewrite it.
