Questions

Does it work without signal on set?

Yes. Each device keeps the full day locally and merges when it sees the network again. Coverage checks from the sup's phone and timing from the AD's iPad reconcile without a server.

Who edits the shot list during the shoot?

Anyone with the day link can mark coverage. Reordering and re-timing is limited to the AD and director roles, so the list stays one list.

Can I keep my spreadsheet?

Export any day as a sheet at any time. Slate writes the columns you'd expect. It just won't be the thing that's current at 2am.

What does it cost?

Free for one project at a time. Productions with a crew list pay per shoot day, not per seat, so the whole set can be on it.
